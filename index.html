About

I'm Jenny — a social media and content marketer focused on overseas markets. I've built Instagram content systems from zero, managed creator rosters of 50+, and run Meta Ads experiments myself — because good content deserves to be seen, and to be measured.

My background in Visual Communication Design shapes how I write briefs: never "list the product features," but "show the moment someone forgets they're wearing your product." I use AI tools across content planning, visual asset generation and campaign analysis — trading technology for time, and time for creative judgment.
